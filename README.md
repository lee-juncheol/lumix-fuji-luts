# Fujifilm Film Simulation LUTs for Panasonic LUMIX

A personal collection of nine film simulation LUTs made for Panasonic LUMIX **Standard (STD) Photo Style**, developed and tested with the **S1M2**.

## How I made these LUTs

I started with Fujifilm’s official LUTs and images shot with the Panasonic LUMIX S1M2 in its Standard (STD) Photo Style. Those gave me a starting point for establishing the color response.

Then I took the camera outside and did the legwork. I deliberately sought out natural shade, backlight, front light, and side light, along with scenes containing greens, yellows, blues, and reds. I photographed them, checked the results, adjusted the LUTs, and went out again.

Each LUT is the result of that hands-on process: real-world shooting, repeated comparisons, and adjustments made one by one. A large part of the work was simply putting in the time outdoors and refining the colors across different lighting conditions and subjects.

## Samples

Classic Negative and Classic Chrome appear first. The nine standalone photographs are grouped using the LUT names recorded in their EXIF metadata. The video captures are grouped using their visible film simulation labels.

The comparison captures include exposure and white-balance matching of the Panasonic images to the Fujifilm reference; some comparisons also include additional color adjustments. They show the finished video comparisons rather than a controlled, unadjusted camera test. Camera labels in each image identify the sides.
